I'm a final-year PhD student in Computer Science at Goethe University Frankfurt, Germany, supervised by Prof. Gemma Roig and Prof. Visvanathan Ramesh. I'm a member of the [CVAI Lab](http://cvai.cs.uni-frankfurt.de) and the [Center for Cognition and Computation](http://www.ccc.cs.uni-frankfurt.de).
 
My research is on multimodal, human-centered machine learning. I build models that recognise human states such as engagement and affect from video, audio, behavioural cues and physiological signals like EEG, GSR, Respiration etc. I study individual differences, identity shortcuts, noisy and subjective labels, and uncertainty, and I look for ways to make these models more reliable for new users.
 
I also work with Honda Research Institute Europe on affective AI. Before my PhD, I spent four years at the SIMIT Lab at Istanbul Technical University working on face biometrics, anomaly detection and signature verification. One of those projects, a signature-verification system built with Yapı Kredi Technologies, is used by one of Turkey's largest banks.
