A systematic study of style-transfer augmentation for domain generalization in driving scene understanding. We separate the effects of the number of styles, texture complexity and the source of the styles. A large and diverse pool of artistic styles helps more than repeating a few styles or using styles from the target domain. From these findings we derive StyleMixDG, a simple, model-agnostic augmentation recipe that needs no architectural changes or extra losses.
